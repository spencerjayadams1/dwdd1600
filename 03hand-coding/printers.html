Printers

There are many kinds of printers that are better at printing certain things. The three you will hear about most are FDM, resin, and SLS machines. The most common and widely used are FDM printers, and they work with the most common materials, which makes them by far the easiest place to start.

Types of Printers

Here is what separates the three technologies:

FDM
Fused Deposition Modeling melts plastic filament and draws each layer. Cheap, forgiving, and the standard choice for a first printer.
Resin
Cures liquid resin with UV light for very fine detail. Excellent for miniatures, but messy and it requires gloves and ventilation.
SLS
Selective Laser Sintering fuses powdered nylon with a laser. Produces strong parts with no supports, but the machines are priced for industry.

Choosing Your First Printer

Work through this list in order before you spend any money. Buy a printer with a heated bed and an active support community, because you will need both in your first month.

  1. Decide what you actually want to print, since detail and size pull in opposite directions.
  2. Measure the space you have, including room for filament spools and ventilation.
  3. Set a budget that includes filament, spare nozzles, and a scraper.
  4. Check that replacement parts for the model are easy to find.
