Welcome to Layer Lair! This is the place where you can learn how to become a 3D printing expert, shop for printer parts, different types of filament, and more! Everything here is written for people who are just opening their first printer box, not for engineers.

Start Here

If you have never printed anything before, follow these four steps in order. Do not skip the leveling step — almost every failed first print traces back to it.

  1. Unbox your printer and run the built in bed leveling tests.
  2. Load a spool of PLA filament and heat the nozzle to 200°C.
  3. Download a test model and slice it with the printer's default profile.
  4. Print a small calibration cube before attempting anything large.

Quick Glossary

You will run into these three words constantly, so it helps to learn them early:

Filament
The spooled plastic that feeds into the printer and becomes your finished object.
Slicer
The software that turns a 3D model into layer-by-layer instructions the printer can follow.
Bed Adhesion
How well the first layer sticks to the print surface, which decides whether the print survives.
