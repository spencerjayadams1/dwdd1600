Materials

There are many kinds of materials you can print with, all with different properties that are used in various situations. Some of the most common 3D printer materials include the following:

PLA
The most common material that is capable of printing toys, 3d models, and holders for light objects.
PETG
Stronger than PLA, is used for more functional things, and in some cases, outdoor things as well.
ABS
Tough and heat resistant, is more prone to warping, letting off a lot of fumes, and can be harder to print with.
ASA
UV and weather resistant, can warp and needs good ventilation when being used to print with, this is best for outdoor parts, some automotive parts, and signs.
TPU
More flexible, but is slower and harder to print with, this can be used to print gaskets, grips, bumpers, feet, etc.
