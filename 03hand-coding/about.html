About Us

Hi! I am Spencer from Layer Lair! We are dedicated to helping you with all of your 3D printing needs, and we are based in Orem, Utah. I personally love printing on my own 3D printer, and I have learned a lot about it from friends, family, and other online resources over the years. The goal of this website is to create a space where beginners can have an easier time getting started on their own 3D printing journey. Happy printing!

What We Believe

Every guide on this site is written around three ideas, because the hardest part of this hobby is the first month, not the machine itself.

  1. Explain the reason behind a setting, not just the number to type in.
  2. Assume the reader owns a budget printer, not a workshop full of equipment.
  3. Admit when a print failed and show what fixed it.
