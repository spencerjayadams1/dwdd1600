Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship is the responsible, respectful, and safe way a person behaves when using technology and participating online.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the rules and laws that govern how people use technology, the internet, and digital information.

What is AUP?

An AUP is a set of rules created by a school, workplace, organization, or other institution that explains how people are allowed to use its technology and network resources.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is presenting someone else's words, ideas, or work as if they were your own without giving them proper credit.

The easiest way to avoid it is to cite your sources and clearly distinguish your own ideas from information you got from others.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is any action that gives a student an unfair advantage in school or misrepresents their academic work.

In simple terms: academic dishonesty means being dishonest about your schoolwork or gaining an unfair academic advantage.

What are the rules for AI Generated Content?

The rules for AI-generated content depend on your school, instructor, or assignment's specific policy. In general, they usually cover when and how you're allowed to use AI tools such as ChatGPT.
